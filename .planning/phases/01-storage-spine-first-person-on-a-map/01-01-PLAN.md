## Phase Goal

**As a** single curator of a private people-and-places dataset, **I want to** connect my own Google Drive, create a person, place them on an image-map, open their profile, and export then restore my whole database, **so that** I own my entire database in my own cloud with no server and can trust the storage spine before I put real data in it.
