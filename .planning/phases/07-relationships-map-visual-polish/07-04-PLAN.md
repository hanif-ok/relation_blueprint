<objective>
Turn the graph's ego emphasis into a dynamic, whole-graph concentric re-layout that follows taps and restores the saved base on exit — without ever overwriting the persisted base positions. This is the POL-03 deliverable, building on Plan 03's grabbable graph and its `suspendSaveRef` save-guard. The single biggest landmine (research Pitfall 1) is that the existing global `layoutstop` auto-save would clobber the base the instant the transient concentric ego runs — the guard must fence it.

Purpose: deliver POL-03 (D-10/D-11/D-12/D-13) by extending GraphView's two existing ego effects into a concentric overlay driven by a local `focusedId`, snapshotting/restoring the base, and adding an Exit-focus control distinct from Reset layout.
Output: dynamic concentric ego focus, transient (base preserved), with Exit focus + the updated e2e spec.
</objective>
