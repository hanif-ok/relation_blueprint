<objective>
Deliver the map-projection vertical slice for REL-03: relationships the user authored appear automatically as data-driven connectors between person-markers on the active map (not hand-drawn) and follow markers live as they move. Connectors are a pure, viewer-only projection (D-10) on a dedicated non-interactive Konva layer beneath the markers (D-08), drawn in the same image-space the markers use so they stay anchored on background re-fit and track drags without per-frame writes.

Purpose: Make authored relationships spatially visible on the map (REL-03) while keeping the canvas clean (labels off by default, D-09) and cheap at scale (listening=false, persist on dragEnd only).
Output: `connectors.ts` pure geometry, `ConnectorLayer.tsx`, the MapView layer insertion + transient drag state, the AvatarMarker onDragMove, the LayersPanel label toggle, and unit + E2E proofs.
</objective>
