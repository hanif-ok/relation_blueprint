|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-03-07 | Denial of Service (memory) | Repeated lightbox open/close + per-tile previews leaking object URLs | mitigate | Reuse the established useMediaUrl/resolveMediaUrl revoke-on-change/unmount lifecycle; one full-res URL at a time |
